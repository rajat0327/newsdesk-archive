Brighter Than White

Modern browsers like Chrome 137+ and Safari 26+ now support ISO 21496-1 gain maps to display HDR content on compatible screens. This technique uses a secondary data map to boost specific image colors beyond standard white levels.

Why it matters — Gain maps allow for high-dynamic-range highlights in web images by using a secondary data layer within standard image formats.

New EU Accessibility Standard EN 301 549 v4.1.1

A new version of the European standard EN 301 549 (Version 4.1.1 (2026-09)) was published on September 2, 2026, and is expected to be published in the Official Journal of the European Union by late November 2026. This updated standard largely aligns with Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 A and AA, replacing the previous Version 3.2.1 (2021-03).

Why it matters — Websites and mobile applications serving European customers must prepare to meet the new EN 301 549 Version 4.1.1, which incorporates WCAG 2.2, as it becomes legally binding by late November 2026.
