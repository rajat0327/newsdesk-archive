NTS: Authenticated Time at Meta

Meta implemented Network Time Security (NTS) on its public time infrastructure to enable authenticated packet verification without in-flight tampering. To scale the service without storage bottlenecks, the servers operate statelessly by deriving cookie keys rather than storing or replicating per-client state.

Why it matters — Deriving session keys cryptographically avoids per-client state storage and replication when scaling authenticated state-heavy protocols.

The keys to the Internet change on October 11. Are you ready?

Cloudflare explains the technical mechanics and operational risk of the DNS root Key-Signing Key (KSK) rollover. Because the key anchors the cryptographic chain of trust for DNSSEC, validating resolvers must trust the new key prior to the switch to prevent resolution failures for valid sites.

Why it matters — Global trust anchor changes require verifying resolver auto-update configurations in advance to prevent widespread DNS resolution outages.
