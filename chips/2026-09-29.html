Xcena Cuts Data Movement to Address Memory Bottlenecks

South Korean startup Xcena is leveraging the Compute Express Link (CXL) protocol to implement near-memory processing. Their architecture aims to reduce data movement between the processor and memory, addressing critical bandwidth bottlenecks in AI applications.

Why it matters — Using CXL to implement near-memory processing as a standard architectural pattern to bypass the memory wall.

NXP-VIS Opens Singapore 300mm Fab

VSMC, a joint venture between NXP Semiconductors and Vanguard International Semiconductor, opened its first 300mm fab in Singapore on September 28, 2026. The fab, which began risk production with over 99% yield on its first sample lot, will produce 130nm-40nm chips and aims for 44,000 300mm wafers per month by 2029, with volume production starting Q1 2027.

Why it matters — This new 300mm fab provides significant additional capacity for mature node specialty chips, securing long-term supply for automotive and industrial sectors starting Q1 2027.
