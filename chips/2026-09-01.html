Advancing The CFET-Based Device Roadmap: Novel Integration Modules And Standard Cell Configurations

The semiconductor industry is preparing to transition to CFET-based transistor architectures starting at the A7 logic technology node. This shift, supported by research from imec, enables standard cells of 4.5-track (4.5T) and below by utilizing novel integration modules and complementary p- and n-type transistor configurations.

Why it matters — Standard cell heights can be reduced to 4.5-track and below by transitioning to CFET architectures at the A7 node and beyond.

M3D 6T SRAM With BEOL Pass-Gates at 2nm (Georgia Tech, Synopsys)

Researchers from Georgia Tech and Synopsys proposed a process-aware monolithic-3D (M3D) 6T SRAM design targeted at the 2nm node. The architecture integrates back-end-of-line (BEOL) indium gallium zinc oxide (IGO) pass-gates with an all-silicon nanosheet latch, buried power rails, and ruthenium (Ru) interconnects.

Why it matters — Monolithic-3D 6T SRAM at the 2nm node can be realized by integrating BEOL IGO pass-gates with an all-silicon nanosheet latch and ruthenium interconnects.
