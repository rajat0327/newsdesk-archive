Japan has offered up to $21 billion to GlobalFoundries to expand its semiconductor complex in the United States. The proposal represents an unprecedented cross-border public-private partnership between the two nations.

Breaking The Memory Bottleneck In AI And HPC SoCs

Embedded memory now consumes over half of total die area in AI and HPC SoCs, making it the primary system bottleneck for bandwidth, latency, and power. The paper explores how embedded memory architectures must adapt alongside gate-all-around (GAA) devices and backside power delivery networks (BSPDN).

Why it matters — SRAM scaling limits require pairing gate-all-around devices and backside power delivery with redesigned embedded memory topologies.

A Composable Thermal Solver For Heterogeneous 2.5D/3D-IC Packages (Cadence, Notre Dame)

Researchers from Cadence and Notre Dame developed a composable AI-accelerated iterative thermal solver for heterogeneous 2.5D and 3D-IC packages. The method reduces thermal modeling runtime compared to full-package finite element method (FEM) simulations that take hours per run.

Why it matters — Replacing monolithic FEM thermal simulations with composable AI solvers significantly accelerates multi-die 3D-IC package heat modeling.

Process & Fabs

TSMC's 2nm Node Achieves Fastest Revenue Ramp

TSMC's 2nm (N2) process node now accounts for 8% of its total wafer revenue in Q3 2026, marking its fastest node ramp on record. This represents TSMC's first volume production using the Gate-All-Around (GAA) nanosheet architecture, transitioning from FinFET, with production sites in Hsinchu Fab 20 and Kaohsiung Fab 22 in Taiwan. TSMC anticipates Q4 2026 revenue to reach between $52.3 billion and $54.3 billion, supported by accelerating 2nm production.

Why it matters — The rapid revenue contribution from TSMC's 2nm GAA nanosheet process validates the new architecture's commercial viability and accelerates its adoption in leading-edge designs.

Memory & Storage

SK hynix Unveils AI Memory, Breaks Ground on Indiana Fab

SK hynix showcased next-generation AI memory technologies at the OCP Global Summit, including MARS (memory-centric AI rack-scale system), High Bandwidth Flash (HBF), and a second-generation 256GB CXL 3.2 memory product offering over twice the bandwidth and 30% better energy efficiency. Concurrently, SK hynix broke ground for an advanced packaging fab in Indiana, US, to strengthen the global semiconductor supply chain.

Why it matters — New memory architectures and localized advanced packaging capacity will enable higher-performance AI systems and diversify the supply chain for critical AI components.
