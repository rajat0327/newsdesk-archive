Process & Fabs

Advancing The CFET-Based Device Roadmap: Novel Integration Modules And Standard Cell Configurations

Industry research indicates a transition to Complementary FET (CFET) architectures starting at the A7 logic node to enable standard cells at or below 4.5 tracks. This involves two R&D tracks: the development of CFET-specific process modules and design-technology co-optimization (DTCO).

Why it matters — Stacking n-type and p-type transistors in a CFET configuration is the primary roadmap for maintaining scaling density when traditional nanosheets hit track-height floorplans.

Design & EDA

Backside Clock Meshes Cut Skew and Power in 2nm Nanosheets (UCSC)

Researchers at UC Santa Cruz are exploring the use of Backside Power Delivery Network (BSPDN) layers to host clock meshes for 2nm GAAFET designs. This approach aims to minimize clock skew and variation while reclaiming premium metal layers on the frontside for signal routing.

Why it matters — Moving clock distribution to the backside of the wafer solves the routing congestion bottleneck on top-tier metal layers in sub-2nm nodes.

China stockpiled 343 immersion DUV tools for advanced chipmaking

Reports indicate China has stockpiled 343 immersion Deep Ultraviolet (DUV) lithography tools to sustain and advance its domestic chipmaking capabilities despite tightening export controls. These tools are critical for multi-patterning techniques used in advanced nodes.

Why it matters — The specific tool count suggests a massive hardware buffer that allows Chinese fabs to continue production and process development even if further equipment sales are completely severed.

Kepler Aims to Launch Energy-Saving Replacement for HBM in 2027

Kepler Computing is targeting a 2027 production date for a new memory technology designed to replace HBM and SRAM in AI chips. The architecture specifically addresses the energy efficiency and bandwidth bottlenecks currently limiting AI accelerator performance.

Why it matters — If successful, this provides an alternative to the high-cost HBM supply chain, which is currently the primary constraint on AI hardware availability.

GPT-Synopsys Combines IC Design EDA with Agentic AI

Synopsys is deploying agentic AI within its EDA tools to automate complex IC design workflows. Unlike previous internal models, these agents can autonomously connect general-purpose LLMs to specific design and verification tools.

Why it matters — Agentic AI shifts EDA from 'AI-assisted' to 'AI-driven' workflows, potentially reducing the engineering man-hours required for physical implementation and verification.

Micron Predicts Worsening RAM Shortages Through 2028

Micron projects RAM and storage chip shortages will worsen through 2027 and 2028, with demand exceeding supply. This forecast comes despite Micron achieving a record 86.25% gross margin and over $53 billion in quarterly profit.
