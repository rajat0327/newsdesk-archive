AI Accelerators Drive Changes In Power Delivery Networks

AI accelerators and GPUs consuming thousands of amperes at ultra-low voltages are pushing hardware architects toward vertical power delivery networks. Integrated voltage regulators and passive components are relocating power delivery closer to silicon loads, expanding simulation requirements and elevating the role of OSAT packaging partners.

Why it matters — Delivering thousands of amperes at low voltages requires shifting to vertical power delivery architectures to mitigate board IR drop and free up substrate area.

Hybrid-Bonded 3D NAND Boosts Analog In-Memory Compute Density (IBM, Micron, U. of Patras)

Researchers from IBM, Micron, and the University of Patras designed a hybrid-bonded 3D NAND flash analog in-memory compute macro. The demonstrated device achieves an analog compute density of 4.8 million weights per square millimeter by integrating logic directly with memory arrays.

Why it matters — Combining 3D NAND with wafer-to-wafer hybrid bonding enables ultra-dense analog in-memory computing by stacking logic directly under non-volatile memory arrays.
