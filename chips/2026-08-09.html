Model Tracks Bond Front Velocity in Lubrication-Mediated Bonding of Flexible Substrates (imec)

An imec researcher has modeled the bond front velocity in lubrication-mediated bonding of flexible substrates. This process is critical for 3D stacking and heterogeneous integration, enabling novel interconnect architectures for integrated circuits.

Why it matters — Quantifying bond front velocity provides a mathematical basis to optimize throughput and yield in lubrication-mediated 3D stacking.

Samsung Unveils zHBM 3D Memory Architecture

Samsung unveiled zHBM, a new 3D memory architecture that vertically stacks HBM directly above AI accelerators, minimizing data travel distance. This design aims to deliver up to eight times the data-processing performance of HBM5 and improve power efficiency by a factor of three.

Why it matters — This new packaging approach could significantly reduce memory bottlenecks and power consumption in future AI systems, enabling denser, more efficient accelerator designs.

US Imposes Tariffs on Polysilicon, Wafers

The US White House imposed a 15% tariff and price floors on polysilicon and 300mm semiconductor wafers, effective December 4, 2026. This measure, under Section 232 of the Trade Expansion Act of 1962, aims to protect domestic polysilicon manufacturers from Chinese competition.

Why it matters — Increased costs for imported polysilicon and wafers will impact upstream material sourcing and potentially raise manufacturing costs for US-based fabs.
