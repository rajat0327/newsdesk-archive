Shrinking Hybrid Bonding Pitches Push Inspection Limits

As hybrid bonding pitches shrink to 5 µm and below, nanometer-scale surface variations and 50 nm particles can lead to yield-killing failures. Achieving high production yield requires CMP processes to strictly control copper recess to within a few nanometers while ensuring strict pre-bond cleanliness.

Why it matters — Sub-5 µm hybrid bonding yield depends heavily on controlling CMP copper recess to just a few nanometers.

Synopsys Expands EDA Access to European Startups

Synopsys has partnered with the European Chips Joint Undertaking (Chips JU) to provide its electronic design automation (EDA) software to semiconductor startups and small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) through the European Chips Design Platform (EuroCDP). This collaboration supports the European Chips Act by offering streamlined access to advanced chip design tools for AI and high-performance computing (HPC) SoC designs on leading-edge process technologies, as well as photonic integrated circuit (PIC) and analog/mixed-signal (AMS) flows.

Why it matters — European startups and SMEs gain access to advanced EDA tools, potentially accelerating innovation and reducing barriers to entry for new chip designs in Europe.
