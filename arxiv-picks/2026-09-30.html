Training & Efficiency

Groupwise Agentic Grading and Advantage Redistribution for Code Agent RL

The authors show that standard Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) gives identical advantage to all test‑passing code trajectories, ignoring differences in implementation quality and task compliance. They propose Groupwise Agentic Grading and Advantage Redistribution, which grades each trajectory on cleanliness and adherence, then redistributes advantage accordingly, giving the policy a richer learning signal.

Why it matters — Provides a way for RL to favor higher‑quality code rather than merely passing tests

Training & Efficiency

SAKI: Maximal-Coupling-Routed Teacher Supervision for On-Policy Distillation

On‑policy distillation suffers when a weak student diverges from the teacher, leading to misaligned supervision. SAKI introduces a KL‑constrained teacher‑guided rollout combined with maximal coupling and an accept/reject reuse scheme, keeping student trajectories close to the teacher while preserving on‑policy exploration.

Why it matters — Keeps student policies aligned with teachers without sacrificing exploration

Systems & Inference

What Makes World Action Models Generalize? An Empirical Study of Test-Time Future Modeling

The paper empirically compares explicit world‑action models that denoise video frames at inference with latent models that skip future generation. Despite the heavy cost of video denoising, latent WAMs achieve comparable performance, demonstrating that discarding explicit future synthesis can retain accuracy while cutting compute.

Why it matters — Shows latent future modeling can match explicit models with far lower inference cost

Training & Efficiency

Selecting Diverse SFT Traces Improves Post-RL Generalization

Investigating supervised‑fine‑tuning (SFT) data for RL, the authors define "route diversity" as variation in reasoning step sequences and devise a lightweight fingerprint to select diverse traces. Experiments reveal that, given equal training budgets, picking the most diverse SFT samples improves post‑RL generalization.

Why it matters — A simple fingerprint‑based selection boosts RL generalization without extra compute

Agents & Tools

Learning Beyond What You Sample: Off-Policy-Aware Cross-Model Trajectory Exchange for RLVR

RL with Verifiable Rewards (RLVR) often encounters rollout groups with no successful trajectories, limiting gradient signals. The authors propose an off‑policy‑aware cross‑model trajectory exchange that reuses successful rollouts discovered by other models, increasing the chance of reward‑based updates under a fixed budget.

Why it matters — Leverages complementary models to fill missing successful rollouts, improving sample efficiency

Multimodal

OmniTaskonomy: When Does Visual Generation Improve Visual Understanding?

The study systematically pairs image‑to‑image generation and image‑to‑text understanding models to ask when visual generation supervision benefits downstream perception tasks. Controlled experiments reveal specific conditions—such as geometry‑rich data and shared encoder architectures—under which generation pre‑training improves understanding.

Why it matters — Identifies concrete scenarios where generation pre‑training yields downstream perception gains
