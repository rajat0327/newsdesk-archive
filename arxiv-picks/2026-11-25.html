Training & Efficiency

cmHC: Balancing Stream Mixing in Hyper-Connections via Geometric Contraction

This work analyzes Manifold-constrained Hyper-Connections (mHC), which extend residual connections across multiple streams by constraining mixing matrices to the Birkhoff polytope. The authors identify a trade-off between residual stream mixing and numerical stability when propagating multi-stream signals.

Why it matters — Constraining multi-stream residual mixing matrices to doubly stochastic polytopes stabilizes gradient propagation across multi-stream architectures without degrading representation mixing.

Training & Efficiency

Composable Soft-Prompt Distillation of RL Experts: Toward Continual Post-Training

Consolidating multiple RL expert models into a single LLM typically requires repeated full-model distillation, risking capability regression on non-target domains. This paper proposes Composable Soft-Prompt Distillation, which modularly combines independently trained RL experts into a unified model via prompt parameters rather than full weight merging.

Why it matters — Soft-prompt distillation enables continual modular integration of specialized RL experts into LLMs without triggering catastrophic forgetting or full-model re-distillation.

Multimodal

Non-penetrating World Models

The authors introduce NoPeBench to evaluate how video world models handle physical obstacle constraints and prevent object penetration. Evaluation shows open-source video models frequently allow interpenetration in generated outputs despite internal representations encoding the underlying spatial physical boundaries.

Why it matters — Physical violations in generative video world models stem primarily from output decoding and sampling failures rather than an absence of internal physical representations.

Agents & Tools

AutoPILOT: A Visual Agent Harness for Real-Time Gameplay

Multimodal agents often fail in unpaused, real-time gaming environments because standard harnesses translate each inference pass into at most one static action. AutoPILOT redesigns the visual agent harness to manage temporal observation caches and output streams of executable actions while the environment continues running.

Why it matters — Real-time agent failure in continuous environments is frequently caused by 1:1 inference-to-action harness execution bottlenecks rather than slow visual model reasoning.

Research

Motion Mamba 2.0: Autoregressive Diffusion Mamba for Long-Horizon Humanoid Control

Autoregressive diffusion models for continuous motion control face high inference overhead from repeated denoising and context limits from bounded attention caches. Motion Mamba 2.0 substitutes attention KV caches with a Mamba state-space backbone to retain distant history during real-time, long-horizon motion generation.

Why it matters — Replacing attention caches with state-space sequence models in autoregressive motion diffusion eliminates quadratic KV cache memory growth during long-horizon control.

Multimodal

Learning Video Foresight Reasoning from Unlabeled Videos via On-Policy Self-Distillation

Predictive visual reasoning in vision-language models typically requires annotations or output from larger teacher models to anticipate future frames. The proposed framework uses on-policy self-distillation to generate supervision directly from unlabeled video streams using student model rollouts.

Why it matters — On-policy self-distillation enables vision-language models to learn temporal foresight reasoning from raw unlabeled video without relying on external teacher annotations.
