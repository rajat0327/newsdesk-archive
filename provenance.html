What is withheld

619 stories are archived; 568 appear on the site. The 51 difference is not editing. It is stories whose link went to a search page, to an expired redirect, to a page that no longer exists, or that repeated another story in the same issue.

The archive keeps all of them. It is the record of what ran, and it is what makes it possible to tell whether any of this is improving.

Links that no longer work

Every published link was checked on 2026-08-23. Of 535, 506 still resolve and 29 answer 404. Those 29 are withheld from the site until they come back; the archive keeps them, because what was published is a matter of record.

Most were never real: a model writing a plausible path on a genuine publication's domain. The check that would have caught them now runs before anything is published, so this number should fall rather than grow.
