Accordion Icons: Which Work Best?

Usability research comparing accordion expansion indicators demonstrates clear performance differences among carets, chevrons, arrows, and triangles. Findings indicate carets are the safest pattern, while split menus and decoupled label-icon actions introduce usability hazards.

Why it matters — Caret icons provide the clearest affordance for accordion expansion, whereas separating actions between text labels and expand icons degrades usability.

UX Practitioners Lukewarm on AI Despite Speed Gains

A Nielsen Norman Group survey of 521 UX practitioners (August 24-September 11, 2026) found AI speeds up tasks (rated 4.2/5 for time saving) but overall sentiment is lukewarm (3.57/5). 69% of open comments expressed negative feelings due to mandates, unreliable output, and inflated expectations. AI use was lowest for tasks involving direct user interaction.

Why it matters — UX teams must address AI output quality and privacy concerns to avoid practitioner dissatisfaction and ensure effective adoption for user-facing tasks.
