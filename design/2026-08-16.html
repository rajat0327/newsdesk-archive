We’re gorging on borrowed trust and it’s going to cost us.

This post explores the user experience challenges of designing onboarding for AI products where users place too much unearned trust in the system. It warns that users are less likely to verify information when it is presented under a trusted brand name, even when the underlying machine is confidently wrong.

Why it matters — Designing AI onboarding requires a tradeoff between leveraging brand trust and preventing users from blindly accepting incorrect machine outputs.

AI & Design

EU Mandates AI Content Labelling

Starting August 2, 2026, new EU guidelines under the AI Act's Article 50 require clear labelling of AI-generated content, including deepfakes, chatbots, and unreviewed public-interest text, for companies serving EU citizens. The guidelines specify using a clearly visible icon with plain text like 'AI-generated' that is accessible and persists across sharing.

Why it matters — Designers of AI-powered interfaces must implement specific, clearly visible, and accessible labelling mechanisms to comply with EU regulations, impacting UI patterns and content.

Smashing Magazine · 16 August 2026 · Read the original →
