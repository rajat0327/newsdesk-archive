10 Lessons Learned from Auditing 100 Online Stores

Audits 100 major e-commerce sites to measure the prevalence of dark patterns such as fake urgency timers and hidden checkout fees. The findings show that up to 68% of audited stores use deceptive mechanisms, which directly correlate with a 70% cart abandonment rate.

Why it matters — Deceptive patterns like fake countdown timers drive cart abandonment up to 70% while eroding long-term brand trust.
