HHS Extends Digital Accessibility Deadlines

The U.S. Department of Health and Human Services (HHS) Office for Civil Rights (OCR) has extended compliance dates for recipients of HHS funding to meet web and mobile accessibility standards under Section 504. Recipients with 15 or more employees now have until May 11, 2027, and those with fewer than 15 employees until May 10, 2028. This one-year extension aligns with similar delays from the Department of Justice.

Why it matters — Organizations receiving HHS funding gain an additional year to conform web content and mobile applications to WCAG 2.1 AA, easing immediate compliance burdens.
