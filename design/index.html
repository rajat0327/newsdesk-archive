Synthetic Users vs. Human Respondents: Same Conclusion, Less Variation

A study comparing AI-generated respondents to 604 human participants in a survey found that both groups led to the same conclusions. However, the AI-generated data exhibited significantly less variation in answers. This research explores the performance of synthetic users as stand-ins for real people in studies.

Why it matters — Synthetic users can yield similar research conclusions to human respondents, potentially offering a method to conduct studies without recruiting real people, though with less answer variation.
