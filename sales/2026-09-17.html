What Would an Independent Slack Look Like If It Hadn’t Sold to Salesforce? And Would It Still Be Worth $27 Billion?

An analysis of what Slack's valuation would be today if it had remained independent estimates it would generate $2.5 billion in ARR with a 25% growth rate. Why it matters — Current SaaS market multiples of 7x-8x ARR for steady-growth companies (25% at scale) highlight the valuation premium Salesforce paid ($27B) compared to Slack's likely independent public market value today.
