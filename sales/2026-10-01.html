How GPU Prices Can Double While AI Gets Cheaper

Despite falling AI prices for consumers, GPU-hour costs have nearly doubled from $4.40 to $8.08 due to infrastructure bottlenecks. The cost of data center components like copper, concrete, and especially electricity are now the primary constraints on AI scaling and long-term pricing.

Why it matters — Electricity and physical infrastructure costs are the new floor for AI pricing, regardless of software-level efficiency gains.
