This post advises startups looking to move upmarket to double their pricing on their next deal rather than experimenting on existing customers. For example, if a company's largest customer currently pays $10k, they should quote $20k to the next prospect to accelerate learning.

Why it matters — Quoting double the price to the very next prospect is a fast, low-risk way to test the upper limits of your product's enterprise value.
