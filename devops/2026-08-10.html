Amazon ECR Supports 200 GB Image Layers

Amazon Elastic Container Registry (ECR) now supports Docker image layers up to 200 GB, a significant increase from the previous limit, allowing users to store larger assets directly within container images.

Why it matters — Teams can now embed large language models or data sets directly into container images, simplifying packaging and eliminating workarounds for large binary dependencies.

Google Distributed Cloud Boosts On-Prem AI

Google Distributed Cloud (GDC) now enables organizations to deploy connected or air-gapped AI models entirely within secure on-premises environments, addressing compliance needs for sensitive data.

Why it matters — Enterprises with strict compliance can now leverage cutting-edge AI models on-premises, mitigating geopolitical risks and avoiding the need to move sensitive data to the public cloud.

Databases & Data Infra

SAP BDC Connect for BigQuery GA

SAP Business Data Cloud (BDC) Connect for BigQuery is now generally available, enabling zero-copy, bi-directional data sharing between SAP systems and Google Cloud's data and AI ecosystem.

Why it matters — Organizations can eliminate data silos and significantly lower analytics costs by directly leveraging SAP data in BigQuery without complex ETL processes, accelerating AI solution deployment.
