Christophe Pettus: All Your GUCs in a Row: recovery_min_apply_delay

Christophe Pettus examines PostgreSQL's recovery_min_apply_delay, a setting that forces a read replica to lag behind the primary intentionally. This artificial delay creates a queryable safety copy where accidental data deletions can be observed and corrected before replay occurs.

Why it matters — Intentionally delaying WAL application on a dedicated standby provides a window to salvage data after destructive queries without restoring full backups.

I don't trust ON DELETE CASCADE

Why it matters — Avoid relying on cascading constraints for large relational tables; explicit batching in application logic prevents lock escalation and write spikes.
