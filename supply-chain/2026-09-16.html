SAP September Patch Day Fixes Critical Flaws

SAP released its September 2026 Patch Day, addressing four critical vulnerabilities, including pre-authentication flaws in the SAP kernel (CVE-2026-44756, CVSS 10.0) and NetWeaver Message Server (CVE-2026-58240, CVSS 9.8). The update also covered credential disclosure in multitenant CAP applications and improper access control in SAP GUI for Java.

Why it matters — Immediate patching is required for core ERP, cloud applications, and client environments to mitigate pre-authentication flaws and other critical vulnerabilities.
