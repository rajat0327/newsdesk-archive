Policy & Regulation

DPDP Act: Consent Manager Portal Opens

The Data Protection Board of India (DPBI) opened registration for Consent Managers under the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023, on November 13, 2026. The Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) launched an online portal for eligible Indian companies to apply. This marks the second phase of the DPDP Act's implementation, with remaining provisions effective May 13, 2027.

Why it matters — Companies processing personal data must now register as Consent Managers or integrate with registered ones, fundamentally changing how user consent is managed and audited.

Manufacturing

India Emphasizes Domestic Semiconductor Manufacturing

Union Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw emphasized on November 14, 2026, the importance of India developing and manufacturing its own machines and high-precision components for semiconductor production. This aligns with the government's focus under Semicon 2.0, which has seen 12 projects approved under Semicon 1.0 with over ₹1.64 lakh crore investment, and five projects commencing commercial production by October 2026.

Why it matters — This policy push signals increased opportunities for domestic companies in the semiconductor equipment and materials sector, fostering a more self-reliant and integrated chip manufacturing ecosystem.

Deep Tech

Raksha Mantri Urges Indigenous Advanced Tech

Raksha Mantri Shri Rajnath Singh, on November 15, 2026, called for India to become a developer, innovator, and global leader in advanced technologies, moving beyond mere adoption. He emphasized strengthening the country's Non-Destructive Testing (NDT) ecosystem to enhance defence preparedness and self-reliance, highlighting the need for indigenous capabilities in inspection technologies, sensors, and software.

Why it matters — This directive signals a strong government push for domestic R&D and manufacturing in critical and deep tech sectors, creating opportunities for Indian companies in defense and aerospace.
