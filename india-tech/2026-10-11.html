Fintech & UPI

UPI Merchant Payments to Incur MDR

Effective October 15, 2026, the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) will implement a 0.4% Merchant Discount Rate (MDR) on person-to-merchant (P2M) UPI transactions exceeding ₹2,000, capped at ₹300. Person-to-person transfers and merchant payments up to ₹2,000 will remain free for consumers.

Why it matters — Merchants accepting high-value UPI payments will incur new transaction costs, potentially influencing their payment acceptance strategies and pricing.

Deep Tech

DRDO Tests High-Altitude Surveillance Airship

On October 6, 2026, the Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO) successfully flight-tested an indigenously developed high-altitude surveillance airship. The platform reached an altitude of 21km and demonstrated capabilities for long-duration operations.

Why it matters — This advances India's strategic surveillance capabilities, offering a cost-effective, long-endurance platform for intelligence gathering and border monitoring.

Manufacturing

ISM 2.0 Boosts Semiconductor Ecosystem

The India Semiconductor Mission (ISM) 2.0, approved on July 15, 2026, with an outlay of ₹1,27,500 crore, expands its focus beyond manufacturing to include R&D, chip design, materials, fabs, talent development, and ATMP/OSAT. This aims to position India as a global semiconductor hub.

Why it matters — This comprehensive strategy and substantial investment create significant opportunities for engineers and businesses in India's burgeoning chip ecosystem.
