DPDP Act Enforcement Nears, Consent Manager Framework Opens

India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act's 'soft enforcement period' is set to conclude in November 2026, with penalties up to INR 2.5 billion for non-compliance for financial firms. Concurrently, the Consent Manager registration framework is expected to operationalize between June and August 2026, requiring businesses to prepare for new data consent management protocols.

Why it matters — Businesses, especially those handling personal data, must prioritize compliance with the DPDP Act as enforcement tightens and the Consent Manager ecosystem becomes active, necessitating robust data governance and privacy engineering solutions.
