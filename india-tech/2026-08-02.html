India's Unified Payments Interface (UPI) processed a record 23.66 billion transactions worth Rs 29.9 lakh crore in July 2026. This significant increase solidifies UPI's position as the preferred digital payment method for millions of Indians and highlights its growing global acceptance.

Semicon India Programme 2.0 Approved with $13.2 Billion Outlay

The Indian Cabinet approved Semicon India Programme 2.0 on July 15, 2026, allocating INR 1.275 trillion (US$13.23 billion) to bolster the country's semiconductor ecosystem. This second phase expands support beyond chip fabrication to include design, advanced packaging, equipment, materials, research, and talent development, with three facilities already in commercial production.

Why it matters — This massive government investment creates significant opportunities for engineers and startups in semiconductor design, manufacturing, and related deep tech areas, aiming to reduce import dependence and establish India as a global chip hub.

Skyroot Aerospace Achieves India's First Private Orbital Launch

Skyroot Aerospace successfully launched its Vikram-1 rocket into orbit on July 18, 2026, marking India's first private company to achieve orbital launch capability. This mission, named 'Aagaman,' carried multiple technology demonstration payloads and signifies a major milestone for India's burgeoning private space sector.

Why it matters — This breakthrough validates India's private space tech capabilities, opening new avenues for deep tech startups in aerospace, satellite technology, and related engineering fields, attracting further investment and talent.

RBI & SEBI Introduce Key Regulatory Changes

The past week saw multiple regulatory updates from RBI and SEBI. RBI released draft FEMA (Foreign Investment) Rules, 2026, and amended directions on interest rates for deposits, effective October 1, 2026. SEBI simplified the framework for securities transmission, mandated ISIN-level freeze of promoter holdings in buy-backs from August 1, 2026, and extended the digital accessibility audit deadline to October 31, 2026.

Why it matters — These regulatory shifts impact financial operations, foreign investment, and market compliance for businesses and investors in India, requiring engineers and founders to adapt their systems and strategies.

PLI 2.0 for Electronics Manufacturing Announced

The India Electronics and Semiconductor Association (IESA) welcomed the government's announcement of PLI 2.0 (MPMS) with an outlay of INR 62,500 crore over five years. This scheme aims to accelerate India's electronics manufacturing momentum, targeting a US$400 billion electronics manufacturing economy by fiscal 2030.

Why it matters — This policy provides substantial incentives for electronics manufacturers, encouraging domestic production and value addition, which is crucial for engineers and companies involved in hardware and electronics development.

DPDP Act Enforcement Nears, Consent Manager Framework Opens

India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act's 'soft enforcement period' is set to conclude in November 2026, with penalties up to INR 2.5 billion for non-compliance for financial firms. Concurrently, the Consent Manager registration framework is expected to operationalize between June and August 2026, requiring businesses to prepare for new data consent management protocols.

Why it matters — Businesses, especially those handling personal data, must prioritize compliance with the DPDP Act as enforcement tightens and the Consent Manager ecosystem becomes active, necessitating robust data governance and privacy engineering solutions.
