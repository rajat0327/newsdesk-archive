Manufacturing

India Begins Chip Packaging

Prime Minister Narendra Modi announced that five chip assembly and packaging plants in India have commenced commercial production. This development is supported by the government's commitment of $13.5 billion for the second phase of the India Semiconductor Mission (ISM 2.0).

Why it matters — India now has domestic commercial production capacity for chip assembly and packaging, reducing reliance on foreign supply chains for electronics manufacturing.

Talent & Hiring

GCCs See Job Shifts

Global Capability Centres (GCCs) in India are projected to cut 25,000-30,000 routine jobs this fiscal year due to AI-led productivity gains and global headcount reductions. Concurrently, 150,000 new tech roles are expected to emerge within GCCs during the same period.

Why it matters — Engineers in routine GCC roles face obsolescence due to AI automation, while those with advanced tech skills will find 150,000 new opportunities in more complex functions.

Deep Tech

QNu Labs Debuts QShield 2.0

QNu Labs launched QShield 2.0, a quantum cybersecurity platform offering modules for quantum entropy, quantum-readiness risk assessment, quantum PKI, quantum vault, and AI security. This follows a February 2026 recommendation for critical infrastructure to adopt post-quantum cryptography by 2027.

Why it matters — Organizations in critical infrastructure must assess and implement post-quantum cryptography solutions like QShield 2.0 by 2027 to comply with new cybersecurity recommendations.
