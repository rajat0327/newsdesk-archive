Policy & Regulation

New IT Rules Mandate 2-Hour Deepfake Takedown

Amendments to the Information Technology (IT) Rules, 2021, notified on February 10, 2026, and effective February 20, 2026, now require social media platforms to remove "sensitive" synthetically generated information (SGI), including deepfakes, within two hours of receiving a complaint. Previously, the deadline was 24 hours. The rules also mandate prominent labeling and embedded metadata for AI-generated content.

Why it matters — Platforms must implement significantly faster content moderation systems and technical measures for AI content, or face legal repercussions for non-compliance.

India Boosts Semiconductor Manufacturing with New Plant, $5B Investment

Commercial production of discrete semiconductor devices commenced at CDIL Semiconductor's Mohali facility on September 17, 2026, coinciding with SEMICON India 2026. At the event, Applied Materials announced a $5 billion investment over the next decade in India for research, supply chain, and workforce development.

Why it matters — India's domestic electronics and deep tech sectors gain a local supply chain for critical components, potentially reducing import reliance and fostering new hardware innovation.

Pixxel Raises $100M Series C

Indian space-tech startup Pixxel secured $100 million in a Series C funding round, led by Singapore state investor Temasek and UK-based Seraphim. Existing investors Radical Ventures and growX Ventures, along with new backers 360 ONE Asset and IMM Investment, also participated. This investment brings Pixxel's total funding to $195 million.

Why it matters — This funding enables Pixxel to expand its satellite network and Earth-observation business, accelerating its shift from hyperspectral imaging to a full Earth-intelligence platform.

Quantum cybersecurity startup QNu Labs raised Rs 200 crore (approximately $20.9 million) in funding. The round saw participation from the National Quantum Mission, Speciale Invest, Sony Innovation Fund, Gaja Capital, and Artha Ventures.

Bodhan AI Debuts Indian Language Models

Bodhan AI, incubated at IIT Madras with AI4Bharat, launched four open models specifically for Indian languages. These models cover speech recognition, speech generation, machine translation, and Optical Character Recognition (OCR), alongside Student Tutor and Teacher Assistant Bots.

Why it matters — Developers and edtech firms can now build localized AI applications and educational tools for Indian languages, expanding digital access and utility.
