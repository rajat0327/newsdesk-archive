Policy & Regulation

New IT Rules Mandate 2-Hour Deepfake Takedown

Amendments to the Information Technology (IT) Rules, 2021, notified on February 10, 2026, and effective February 20, 2026, now require social media platforms to remove "sensitive" synthetically generated information (SGI), including deepfakes, within two hours of receiving a complaint. Previously, the deadline was 24 hours. The rules also mandate prominent labeling and embedded metadata for AI-generated content.

Why it matters — Platforms must implement significantly faster content moderation systems and technical measures for AI content, or face legal repercussions for non-compliance.

QNu Labs launched QShield 2.0, a quantum cybersecurity platform offering modules for quantum entropy, quantum-readiness risk assessment, quantum PKI, quantum vault, and AI security. This follows a February 2026 recommendation for critical infrastructure to adopt post-quantum cryptography by 2027.

Why it matters — Organizations in critical infrastructure must assess and implement post-quantum cryptography solutions like QShield 2.0 by 2027 to comply with new cybersecurity recommendations.

ISM 2.0 Boosts Semiconductor Ecosystem

The India Semiconductor Mission (ISM) 2.0, approved on July 15, 2026, with an outlay of ₹1,27,500 crore, expands its focus beyond manufacturing to include R&D, chip design, materials, fabs, talent development, and ATMP/OSAT. This aims to position India as a global semiconductor hub.

Why it matters — This comprehensive strategy and substantial investment create significant opportunities for engineers and businesses in India's burgeoning chip ecosystem.

Policy & Regulation

DPDP Act Clarifies Consent, Data Audit Terms

The Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) issued the Digital Personal Data Protection (Removal of Difficulties) Order, 2026, on October 5, 2026, effective upon gazette publication on October 7. The order clarifies Section 9(1) regarding verifiable consent for processing personal data of persons with disabilities who have lawful guardians, and amends Section 10(2)(c)(ii) by replacing 'audit' with 'data audit' for Significant Data Fiduciaries.

Why it matters — Businesses must ensure consent procedures and audit frameworks reflect the clarified language for persons with disabilities and explicitly use 'data audit' for SDF obligations.

Fintech & UPI

UPI MDR Rollout Deferred Post-Diwali

The National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) has deferred the implementation of a 0.4% Merchant Discount Rate (MDR) on person-to-merchant (P2M) UPI transactions above ₹2,000 until after Diwali 2026. This charge was initially expected to take effect from October 15, 2026. Person-to-person transfers and P2M transactions up to ₹2,000 remain free, with specific caps and flat fees for certain essential services and capital market transactions.

Why it matters — Merchants will not incur the 0.4% MDR on P2M transactions above ₹2,000 until after Diwali, providing a temporary reprieve from increased transaction costs.
