Fintech & UPI

RBI Expands UPI Transaction Limits

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) announced several UPI limit enhancements on November 7. These include increasing the UPI transaction limit for tax payments from ₹1 lakh to ₹5 lakh, raising the UPI 123Pay limit to ₹10,000, and increasing the UPI Lite wallet limit to ₹5,000 with a ₹1,000 per-transaction limit.

Why it matters — These changes directly expand the utility of UPI for higher-value transactions in specific categories and for feature phone users, making digital payments more versatile for a broader user base.

Fintech Unicorn Razorpay Gets SEBI IPO Nod

Fintech unicorn Razorpay received approval from SEBI for its proposed Initial Public Offering (IPO), as reported on November 7. The Bengaluru-based company had confidentially filed its DRHP in June 2026 and aims to raise ₹5,500-6,500 crore at an expected valuation of $5 billion.

Why it matters — This marks a significant step towards public listing for a major Indian fintech player, providing a potential liquidity event for early investors and setting a valuation benchmark for other private fintechs.
