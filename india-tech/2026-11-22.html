India Joins Pact to Secure AI Supply Chains

India, the U.S., and eight other nations signed the San Francisco Declaration on November 15, 2026, committing to safeguard Artificial Intelligence (AI) supply chains and critical technologies. This agreement, part of the U.S.-led Pax Silica initiative, aims to protect semiconductors, critical minerals, and related infrastructure from disruptions.

Why it matters — This formalizes India's role in global efforts to secure critical AI and semiconductor supply chains, impacting future technology partnerships and trade.

India Opens Space Economy to Private Sector

The Government of India, through the Indian Space Policy 2023, has opened the entire space value chain to Non-Government Entities, with IN-SPACe as the single-window interface and NSIL as the commercial arm. This policy, effective November 15, 2026, aims to build a collaborative, industry-led, and globally competitive space ecosystem.

Why it matters — This policy shift creates significant opportunities for private companies and startups in India's space sector, fostering innovation and reducing reliance on state-led initiatives.
