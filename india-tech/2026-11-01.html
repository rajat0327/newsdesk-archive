Manufacturing

Textiles PLI Scheme Reopens

The Ministry of Textiles reopened the application portal for Round 4 of the Production Linked Incentive (PLI) Scheme for Textiles from November 1 to December 31, 2026. Key modifications include reducing the minimum turnover criterion from ₹200 crore to ₹100 crore for Part 2 and extending the scheme tenure to FY 2033-34 for Round 4 applicants.

Why it matters — Smaller manufacturing units (MSMEs) can now more easily access incentives to boost domestic production of Man-Made Fibre apparel, fabrics, and technical textiles.

India to Draft Data Centre Policy

India is preparing a new policy for data centers, announced by Junior IT Minister Jitin Prasada, in response to growing concerns over the sector's electricity and water consumption. This follows protests against planned data center projects by Amazon and Google in Mumbai and Visakhapatnam.

Why it matters — New regulations could impose stricter environmental and resource consumption standards on data center operators, potentially increasing operational costs or influencing site selection.

Fintech & UPI

UPI Daily Transactions Hit ₹1 Trillion

The average daily value of Unified Payments Interface (UPI) transactions surpassed ₹1 trillion for the first time in October 2026. During the month, UPI processed 25.47 billion transactions, a 23% increase from October 2025, with an average daily volume of 822 million transactions.
