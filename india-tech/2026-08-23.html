Sarvam AI Debuts LLM Platform

Bengaluru-based startup Sarvam AI launched Epoch Builder Edition, a developer and enterprise platform designed to help organizations build, fine-tune, and deploy large language models (LLMs) tailored for Indian languages and use cases. The platform features multilingual LLMs (7B and 70B parameters) supporting over 10 Indian languages.

Why it matters — Indian developers and enterprises gain access to a specialized platform and pre-trained models for building AI applications with strong support for local languages, accelerating India-centric AI innovation.
