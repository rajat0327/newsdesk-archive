TakeMe2Space Launches Orbital Computing Satellite

Indian space startup TakeMe2Space plans to launch its MOI-1A orbital computing satellite, carrying Nvidia Orin NX edge-computing processors, aboard a SpaceX rocket on October 1, 2026. The satellite aims to process data in orbit, reducing the need to transmit raw information back to Earth.

Why it matters — This enables customers to upload containerized AI models for in-orbit data processing, significantly reducing data download costs and latency for space-based analytics.

Simple Energy Raises $180M Series C

Bengaluru-based electric two-wheeler manufacturer Simple Energy raised $180 million (₹1,750 crore) in an all-equity Series C round on September 30, 2026. The funding was led by the family office of Thyrocare founder Arokiaswamy Velumani, with participation from other investors.

Why it matters — This significant capital infusion will enable Simple Energy to scale its production and market presence in the competitive Indian electric vehicle sector.

ECMS Outlay Increased to ₹40,000 Crore

The Union Budget 2026–27 increased the outlay for the Electronics Components Manufacturing Scheme (ECMS) to ₹40,000 crore. This enhanced allocation aims to further deepen domestic electronics component manufacturing and strengthen India's electronics supply chain.

Why it matters — The increased funding will incentivize greater domestic production of critical electronic components, reducing import dependence and boosting India's manufacturing capabilities.
