DPDP Act's Consent Manager Phase Begins Nov 2026

Phase 2 of India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, specifically the Consent Manager framework under Rule 4, will become operational on November 13, 2026. This mandates that Data Fiduciaries must be technically ready to integrate with registered Consent Managers. Full substantive compliance and active financial enforcement, with penalties up to ₹250 crore per violation, are set for May 13, 2027.

Why it matters — Organizations processing Indian personal data must integrate with Consent Managers by November 2026 to avoid compliance failures and potential penalties, requiring significant technical and operational adjustments.
