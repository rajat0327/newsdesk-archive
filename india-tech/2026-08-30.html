India's Tech Sector Sees Policy Implementation, Space Innovation, and Robust Startup Funding

One week of India Tech Weekly, 4 stories, as published.

Policy & Regulation

Government Mandates DPDP Act Compliance

Cabinet Secretary T.V. Somanathan directed Union ministries, state governments, and Union Territory administrations to create time-bound implementation plans for the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023. This directive, issued in August, initiates a government-wide effort to identify personal data, examine processing methods, and establish institutional accountability for compliance, following the notification of DPDP Rules in November 2025.

Why it matters — Government departments must now actively map citizen data, strengthen privacy protocols, and assign clear accountability for data storage, access, sharing, and erasure.

Deep Tech

Indian Startup Plans Space Pharma Manufacturing

A young CEO of an Indian space startup, Antariksha, informed Prime Minister Narendra Modi on August 21, 2026, about their venture to develop satellites capable of manufacturing pharmaceuticals in space and returning them to Earth. The company claims to be India's first space-pharma startup.

Why it matters — This initiative could establish India as a pioneer in space-based pharmaceutical production, opening new avenues for drug discovery and manufacturing beyond Earth's gravity.

Fintech & UPI

UPI Expands Limits, Global Reach

On August 24, 2026, UPI introduced "UPI Circle" for secondary users with predefined transaction limits. RBI increased the UPI transaction limit for tax payments from ₹1 lakh to ₹5 lakh, extending this to capital markets, IPOs, loan collections, insurance, healthcare, and education. The per-transaction limit for UPI 123Pay was raised to ₹10,000, and the UPI Lite wallet limit increased to ₹5,000, with a ₹1,000 per-transaction limit. UPI is now live in 11 countries, with Greece and the Maldives recently adopting it for cross-border remittances.

Why it matters — Users gain more flexibility and higher transaction limits for various payments, while UPI's international expansion simplifies cross-border remittances for Indians abroad.

Funding

Indian Startups Secure Key Funding Rounds

Between August 24-29, 2026, several Indian startups secured significant investments. Third Wave Coffee raised Rs 408 crore (approx. $49M) in a round led by WestBridge Capital. Skincare brand Asaya secured Rs 88 crore (approx. $10.6M) in a Series A round led by RPSG Capital. Deeptech company WATER raised $2.5 million led by Endiya Partners, and digital spiritual wellness platform InstaAstro secured $12 million in a Series A round led by Singularity AMC and Artha Venture Fund.
