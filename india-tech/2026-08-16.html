Manufacturing

India Expands Semiconductor, Electronics Manufacturing

Prime Minister Narendra Modi announced that three semiconductor plants (Micron, Kaynes, CG Semi) have begun commercial production under Semicon 1.0, with 5-8 more expected in the coming years. The Semicon 2.0 program, approved in July 2026 with a ₹1,27,500 crore outlay, further strengthens India's semiconductor design and manufacturing ecosystem. The PLI scheme for Large Scale Electronic Manufacturing has attracted ₹96,000 crore in investments.

Why it matters — Increased domestic chip and electronics production reduces import dependence and creates opportunities for local suppliers and skilled manufacturing jobs.

Deep Tech

India's Space Economy Reaches $9 Billion

India's space economy is valued at $9 billion as of August 2026, projected to reach $40-45 billion within the next decade. Private investment surged to $618.5 million by March 2026, with $187 million in 2026 alone, and registered space startups grew to 440.

Why it matters — This growth signals increasing opportunities for private players in launch services, satellite operations, and deep tech, shifting the ecosystem from state-led to collaborative.

Funding

Mirae Asset, Bluehill.VC Close New Funds

Mirae Asset Venture Investments (MAVI) announced the first close of Rs 1,125 crore for its Rs 1,800 crore Venture Opportunity Fund – II. Separately, Chennai-based Bluehill.VC closed its maiden Rs 400 crore frontier-tech fund, including a Rs 50 crore greenshoe option.

Why it matters — These new funds provide capital for Indian startups, particularly in frontier tech, enabling further innovation and growth in the ecosystem.

Big Tech in India

Google, IBM Launch AI Initiatives in India

Google partnered with Lightspeed for an 'AI Agent Sprint' to help consumer tech and fintech startups build AI agents. IBM also launched an AI Lab in India with a high-performance computing facility to democratize access to advanced AI resources for students, researchers, and institutions.

Why it matters — These initiatives provide critical infrastructure and expertise, accelerating AI development and adoption for Indian startups and researchers.
