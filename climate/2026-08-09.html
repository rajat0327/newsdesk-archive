Data Centre Demand

Data Centers Strain Grids, Prompt Moratoriums

Global electricity demand from data centers grew 17% in 2025, with AI-focused centers up 50%, leading to significant grid strain. In Q1 2026, 75 U.S. data center projects worth $130 billion were blocked or delayed due to energy constraints and delayed grid connections. New York imposed a one-year moratorium on new data center construction in mid-July, and PJM accepted 715 new generation projects (over 200 GW) into its reformed interconnection process to address supply shortages.

Why it matters — Grid operators and developers must now factor in significant regulatory and financial risks, including potential moratoriums and higher costs for grid upgrades, when planning new data center projects.

Multiple Advanced Reactors Achieve Criticality

Oklo's Groves Isotope Test Reactor achieved criticality on August 6, 2026, in Lockhart, Texas, becoming the fifth DOE-authorized advanced reactor to reach this milestone this summer. This follows Antares Nuclear's Mark-0 (June 4), Valar Atomics' Ward 250 (June 18), Deployable Energy's Unity (June 30), and Aalo Atomics' Aalo-X (July 4) also achieving criticality, as reported in an August 4, 2026, DOE fact sheet.

Why it matters — These milestones demonstrate the successful, safe operation of new nuclear reactor designs, accelerating the pathway to commercial deployment and contributing to grid decarbonization.
