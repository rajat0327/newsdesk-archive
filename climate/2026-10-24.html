Google plans big virtual power plant in energy-strapped PJM grid

Google and Voltus have partnered to aggregate 1 gigawatt of virtual power plant capacity across the PJM Interconnection grid. The project targets harnessing flexible demand from commercial and residential devices to relieve grid congestion and clear room for new data center loads.

Why it matters — Aggregating flexible customer loads into a gigawatt-scale VPP offers a near-term mechanism to relieve transmission bottlenecks for rapid data center expansion.

ANALYSIS: Solar & Storage Growth Drives Down Summer Wholesale Electricity Prices by 42% in Texas & 51% in California

An analysis from the Solar Energy Industries Association shows that solar and battery storage deployment drove down summer wholesale electricity prices by 42% in ERCOT (Texas) and 51% in CAISO (California). The figures demonstrate how renewables and storage mitigate wholesale power spikes during peak summer demand.

Why it matters — Grid-scale solar combined with battery storage dampens peak wholesale power prices during extreme summer heat waves across distinct ISO market structures.

Can distributed energy work around American utilities?

This post analyzes why virtual power plants have scaled rapidly in Britain via Octopus Energy while facing severe roadblocks in the United States. It outlines how fragmented utility structures, lack of wholesale market integration, and legacy utility business models prevent American distributed batteries and smart devices from acting as grid assets.

Why it matters — Monopolistic utility revenue models and lack of standardized real-time market access present bigger bottlenecks to VPP scaling than consumer hardware adoption.

Gas Network Retirement Is Becoming A Real Utility Planning Tool

Gas distribution utilities face structural infrastructure trade-offs as building electrification progresses piecemeal while main gas lines remain operational. To avoid stranded assets and unsustainable rate increases on remaining customers, utility planners are beginning to design targeted, section-by-section gas pipe retirements.

Why it matters — Pruning gas distribution networks section-by-section is structurally necessary to avoid severe rate spirals as customer electrification scales down gas demand unevenly.

As US grid batteries surge, risk of fires plummets

Data on grid battery deployments in the US indicates that despite rapid growth in utility-scale capacity, the risk and frequency of battery fire incidents per megawatt-hour installed have dropped significantly. Improvements in system engineering, safety standards, and operational controls are helping resolve community siting concerns.

Why it matters — Operational experience and updated design standards have sharply reduced failure rates per installed MWh even as grid storage volumes scale rapidly.

Revealed: Expensive gas behind ‘nearly 90%’ of rise in UK electricity bills

Analysis by thinktank Nesta demonstrates that high wholesale fossil gas costs caused nearly 90% of the increase in UK electricity bills and 77% of gas bill increases since 2020. The findings counter arguments that renewable energy subsidies were the primary driver of recent energy cost inflation.

Why it matters — Marginal-cost power pricing models mean fossil gas prices dictate overall wholesale electricity rates even in grids with high renewable penetration.
