Shuttered nuclear plant in Iowa gets $1.9B federal loan to restart

NextEra Energy secured a federal loan commitment of up to $1.9 billion from the U.S. Department of Energy to restart the Duane Arnold Energy Center in Iowa. This marks the third shuttered U.S. nuclear power plant attempting a comeback with federal financial backing.

Why it matters — A $1.9 billion federal loan commitment demonstrates the government's willingness to underwrite the high capital costs of resurrecting retired nuclear capacity to meet growing clean energy demand.

Is there way more conventional geothermal than we thought?

Geothermal startup Zanskar is using artificial intelligence to locate undiscovered hydrothermal systems that lack surface indicators. The company has drilled three sites to validate its technology, including a successful 'blind' discovery in Nevada.

Why it matters — Using machine learning to identify 'blind' hydrothermal systems can unlock vast, previously hidden baseload geothermal resources without relying on surface thermal signatures.
