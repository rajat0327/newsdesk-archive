ANALYSIS: Solar & Storage Growth Drives Down Summer Wholesale Electricity Prices by 42% in Texas & 51% in California

An analysis from the Solar Energy Industries Association shows that solar and battery storage deployment drove down summer wholesale electricity prices by 42% in ERCOT (Texas) and 51% in CAISO (California). The figures demonstrate how renewables and storage mitigate wholesale power spikes during peak summer demand.

Why it matters — Grid-scale solar combined with battery storage dampens peak wholesale power prices during extreme summer heat waves across distinct ISO market structures.

The DOE announces $5B loan to Constellation for nuclear uprates

The U.S. Department of Energy announced a $5 billion conditional loan to Constellation to uprate 15 reactors at eight nuclear plants across Illinois, Maryland, and Pennsylvania. The project aims to increase total carbon-free output by 1.1 GW through technical upgrades to existing infrastructure.

Why it matters — Uprating existing nuclear assets can provide 1.1 GW of carbon-free capacity faster and more cost-effectively than new-build projects.

PJM desperately needs grid batteries. Here’s a way it could get some.

PJM Interconnection, the largest US grid operator, is implementing reforms to its interconnection process to accelerate the integration of battery storage. The move aims to address severe congestion and delays across its 13-state territory.

Look Ma, No Inverter! New Ford Fathom Cuts The Cost Of Bidirectional EV Charging

Ford's upcoming Fathom electric truck integrates bidirectional charging hardware directly into the vehicle, removing the need for a separate, expensive external inverter for home backup power. This technical shift aims to lower the total cost of ownership for vehicle-to-home (V2H) systems.

Why it matters — Integrating the inverter into the vehicle chassis removes a major capital cost barrier for bidirectional grid services.
