China Truck Sales Now Majority Electric. What’s Next?

EV heavy trucks achieved a 50.3% market share in China for September, with sales up 34% from a year ago, and long-haul big rig market share hitting 64.5%. China is on track to have over 40% EV market share for the entire year, reaching its 2030 goal in a matter of months.

Why it matters — China's rapid electrification of heavy-duty and long-haul trucking, reaching over 50% market share, provides a real-world case study of accelerated fleet decarbonization and its potential.

German startup Revolta commissioned its first 10 kWh sodium-ion home battery system at a customer property near Aschaffenburg, Germany, on November 5, 2026. The high-voltage system operates at a nominal voltage of 450 V and comprises five 2 kWh battery modules.
