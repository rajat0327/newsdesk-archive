ANALYSIS: Solar & Storage Growth Drives Down Summer Wholesale Electricity Prices by 42% in Texas & 51% in California

An analysis from the Solar Energy Industries Association shows that solar and battery storage deployment drove down summer wholesale electricity prices by 42% in ERCOT (Texas) and 51% in CAISO (California). The figures demonstrate how renewables and storage mitigate wholesale power spikes during peak summer demand.

“Repowering” worn out solar fields could create gigawatts of new grid capacity, quickly

America’s solar and battery installations built 15–20 years ago are facing declining performance from failing inverters and expiring power contracts. Lisa Hansmann of Foundry-Logic details how repowering these existing operational sites with modern panels and upgraded storage offers a faster, cheaper path to grid capacity than waiting on new interconnection queues.

Why it matters — Repowering existing interconnected sites bypasses multi-year interconnection queue delays to deliver rapid grid capacity increases.

Google bets on superhot geothermal to power Oregon data centers

Google has agreed to purchase 50 megawatts of carbon-free electricity from Quaise Energy's first commercial superhot geothermal project, Project Obsidian, near Newberry, Oregon. The off-take agreement aims to provide firm, zero-carbon power directly to Google's power-dense Oregon data centers.

Why it matters — Superhot geothermal is moving into commercial off-take contracts to deliver firm, zero-carbon baseload energy for high-load data centers.
