ANALYSIS: Solar & Storage Growth Drives Down Summer Wholesale Electricity Prices by 42% in Texas & 51% in California

An analysis from the Solar Energy Industries Association shows that solar and battery storage deployment drove down summer wholesale electricity prices by 42% in ERCOT (Texas) and 51% in CAISO (California). The figures demonstrate how renewables and storage mitigate wholesale power spikes during peak summer demand.

Why it matters — Grid-scale solar combined with battery storage dampens peak wholesale power prices during extreme summer heat waves across distinct ISO market structures.

Transport & EV

Europe’s Inland Shipping Models Underprice Green Fuels. Market Prices Favor Battery Swapping.

An analysis of inland shipping economics shows that using real-world market prices (€2,515/day for hydrotreated vegetable oil) makes battery swapping €551/day cheaper for a standard 110-meter dry-cargo vessel. Incorporating fuel delivery costs into financial models significantly alters the cost comparison against theoretical projections.

Why it matters — Delivered fuel market pricing flips the operational cost advantage from bio-fuels to direct battery swapping for inland waterway freight.

The truth about data centers and electricity prices

A report models the financial impact of large data center loads on electric grids, finding retail electricity rates can swing between a 6% decrease and a 9% increase. The financial outcome depends heavily on utility cost-allocation rules and tariff structures set by utility regulators.

Why it matters — Large industrial load additions only reduce system-wide electricity rates if regulators force load growth developers to fund transmission upgrades upfront.

Industry & Heat

Adani Cement to Deploy 410 Electric Trucks, Cement & Clinker

Adani Cement is deploying 410 electric trucks by March 2027 to transport cement and clinker from its Sanghi Plant in Gujarat. The fleet rollout aims to eliminate over 25,000 tonnes of annual CO2 emissions as part of a target to electrify 10% of logistics volume by 2030.

Why it matters — Heavy industrial logistics can achieve rapid carbon reductions by targeting point-to-point material haulage routes for early fleet electrification.

Grid & Transmission

Utilities Build Record 21,000 km Grid

Members of the Utilities for Net Zero Alliance (UNEZA) reported building over 21,000 kilometers of new grid in 2025, a 50% increase from 2024. The alliance also committed to annual investments of at least US$156 billion across grids, renewables, and storage projects, aiming to mobilize over US$1 trillion by 2030.

Why it matters — The record 21,000 km of new grid built in 2025 and the $156 billion annual investment commitment demonstrate a significant, measurable acceleration in global grid expansion for clean energy.
