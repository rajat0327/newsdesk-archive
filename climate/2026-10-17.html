Denmark has reduced emissions by nearly half since 1990 using wind and renewables, but analysts doubt it will achieve its 70% target by 2030. Green Power Denmark CEO Kristian Jensen argues that closing the gap requires shifting focus to electrifying direct energy uses across transport, heat, and industry.

US Clean Energy Surges, Gas Also Expands Amid Data Center Boom

The US is projected to add a record 89 GW of clean energy in 2026, with an even higher figure expected in 2027, driven by expiring federal tax credits and surging data center demand. However, this boom is also fueling a significant expansion of natural gas, with 67.8 GW of new gas-fired generation projected by 2030 against only 14.3 GW of retirements. Data center capacity is now expected to reach 118 GW by 2030, a 52% increase from previous forecasts.

Why it matters — The rapid growth of data centers is creating a dual effect, accelerating both renewable energy deployment and new fossil gas capacity, complicating grid decarbonization efforts.
