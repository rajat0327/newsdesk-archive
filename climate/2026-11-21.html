Battery Storage Costs Show Wide Range by Scale

Installed battery energy storage system (BESS) prices in 2026 range from $100–150 per kWh for utility-scale, $250–450 per kWh for commercial & industrial, and LFP battery modules alone cost $140–240 per kWh. Larger systems achieve lower per-kWh costs due to economies of scale.
