Moonshot AI Releases Kimi K3.1 Pro Model

Moonshot AI released Kimi K3.1 Pro on October 23, 2026, a 3-trillion-parameter open-weight model with a 1-million-token context window, and a faster Highspeed variant. It achieved 65.7% on Terminal-Bench 4.0, outperforming GPT-6 Astra, and is priced at $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output, a third less than Kimi K3.

Why it matters — Developers can now access a near-frontier open-weight model with a large context window at a significantly reduced API cost for complex agentic tasks.
