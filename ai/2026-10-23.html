“We were looking at it wrong”: The explosive claim that’s upended the AI world

Goodfire researcher Nick Cammarata published a 26,000-word analysis claiming that neural networks are significantly easier to interpret than previously assumed. The findings suggest that large language models may be far simpler to understand internally than current consensus holds.

Why it matters — If neural network mechanics are simpler to decode than expected, safety auditing and interpretability tools can be integrated directly into model evaluation workflows.

llm-echo 0.5

Flint-interactive: A language for creating interactive data visualizations

Microsoft Research developed Flint-interactive, a high-level language enabling AI agents to generate interactive data visualizations. Users can explore generated charts via clicking, filtering, zooming, and cross-chart interactions without underlying custom code.

Why it matters — Abstracting low-level chart code into high-level declarative interaction primitives enables agents to generate complex UI elements reliably.
