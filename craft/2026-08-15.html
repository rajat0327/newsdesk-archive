Canva Shares S3 Based Architecture for Session Revocation across Hundreds of Millions of Sessions

Canva redesigned its session revocation infrastructure to support 100 million active sessions while reducing database lookups. The new architecture utilizes Amazon S3 for durable revocation records and distributes compact, in-memory indexes to application gateways.

Why it matters — Distributing compact, in-memory indexes to gateways from S3 cut Canva's revocation cache memory footprint by 87.5% and reduced database infrastructure requirements.

How Netflix Scaled its Real-Time Service Map

Netflix redesigned the streaming pipeline behind its real-time Service Topology map to handle production scale. The new system separates intermediary resolution, enrichment, and persistence into three distinct stages, propagates backpressure to Kafka, and uses server-sent events instead of gRPC for internal transfers.

Why it matters — Propagating backpressure to Kafka instead of dropping records, combined with using server-sent events for high-volume internal transfers, allows the pipeline to scale reliably without data loss.
