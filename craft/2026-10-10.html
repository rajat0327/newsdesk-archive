Grab Redesigns Counter Service Storage for 50% Lower P99 Latency

Grab migrated its high-volume Counter Service from a wide-column database to Aerospike using storage abstraction and shadow traffic for validation. The move consolidated time buckets into map-based records, resulting in 1 TB of disk usage compared to the previous 3 TB.

Why it matters — Shadow traffic and data parity validation enable safe, high-volume database migrations while reducing cost per node by up to 50%.
