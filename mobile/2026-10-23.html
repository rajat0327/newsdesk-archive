Privacy

A more reliable way to add and edit contacts on Android

Apps targeting the next Android release will lose the ability to write directly to the contacts database via the WRITE_CONTACTS permission. Developers must transition to new, mediated APIs for adding or editing contact information.

Why it matters — Direct write access to the contacts provider is being phased out, forcing a migration to more restricted, system-managed APIs for contact creation and editing.

Performance

Grab Reduces ANRs by 32% and improves startup time by 35% with R8 and Profile guided optimization

Grab improved its superapp performance by utilizing R8 optimizations and Profile Guided Optimization (PGO). These changes resulted in a 32% reduction in ANRs and a 35% improvement in cold start times across eight countries.

Why it matters — Profile Guided Optimization (PGO) can significantly reduce ANRs and startup latency in complex apps by optimizing the most frequently executed code paths.

Android Developers · 23 October 2026 · Read the original →

Cross-Platform

React Native 0.88 - Web-Based Event Dispatching, Variable Fonts, SceneDelegate, no breaking changes

React Native 0.88 introduces web-based event dispatching by default and adds support for variable fonts on both iOS and Android. This release also includes SceneDelegate lifecycle support for iOS and updates the framework to React 19.3.0.

Why it matters — The transition to web-based event dispatching aligns React Native's event loop more closely with browser standards, improving cross-platform consistency.

Store & Policy

Updated App Review Guidelines are now available

Apple has updated its App Review Guidelines, specifically Guideline 1.2, to clarify content requirements and developer responsibilities. The update notes that failing to implement proper safeguards against prohibited content can lead to Developer Code of Conduct violations.

Why it matters — App Store compliance now explicitly links content moderation failures to potential developer account termination under the Code of Conduct.

Store & Policy

Offer your subscriptions to businesses and schools

Apple now allows organizations and schools to purchase auto-renewable subscriptions in volume through Apple Business Manager and Apple School Manager. This multiseat purchasing system enables organizations to manage and assign subscription seats via a single in-app purchase.

Why it matters — This feature enables a centralized B2B/Education billing model for subscriptions, removing the need for individual user-level in-app purchases in organizational settings.

Apple Developer · 23 October 2026 · Read the original →

Store & Policy

Strengthening App Review Guidelines against nonconsensual intimate imagery and bait-and-switch apps

Apple is strengthening its guidelines against nonconsensual intimate imagery and deceptive bait-and-switch apps. The update explicitly prohibits AI-generated nudity and emphasizes consistent enforcement of standards against sexually explicit material.

Why it matters — Apple is increasing scrutiny on generative AI apps to prevent the creation of sexually explicit content and deceptive marketing practices.

Tooling

Bringing our latest frontier model to Android Developers

Google is evolving Android development tools from assistive code completion to agentic AI capable of executing end-to-end tasks. This shift allows developers to direct agents to perform complex engineering workflows within the IDE.

Why it matters — The transition from assistive to agentic AI suggests a shift in how mobile engineers will handle boilerplate and complex refactoring.
