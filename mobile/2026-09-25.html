iOS & Swift

Build for iPhone Duo with new resources

Apple has released Xcode 27.1 beta and updated design resources to support the new iPhone Duo hardware form factor. The release includes new Figma and Sketch kits specifically for optimizing app layouts across multiple screen sizes.

Why it matters — The introduction of new hardware configurations requires a shift from fixed layouts to flexible, size-class-based architectures that can adapt to dual-screen environments.

Android & Kotlin

Land your apps on Googlebook with adaptive development

Googlebook is a new category of laptop hardware from partners like Dell and HP that runs on a shared Android foundation. These devices feature high-resolution OLED touchscreens and dedicated keyboards, requiring apps to support desktop-class input and adaptive layouts.

Why it matters — The expansion of Android into dedicated laptop hardware forces developers to account for persistent physical keyboard input and high-resolution desktop-style display metrics.

Build your way: Use any AI agent of your choice in Android Studio

Android Studio now supports the integration of third-party AI coding agents and custom enterprise harnesses. This allows development teams to use their preferred autonomous tools and specialized AI multipliers directly within the IDE.

Why it matters — Decoupling the IDE from a specific AI provider allows engineering teams to standardize their agentic workflows across different platforms and languages.

Bring your Android game to the car screen today

The games category for Android Auto and Android Automotive OS has graduated from beta to general availability. This allows developers to ship parked-only gaming experiences to vehicles using standard Android development tools.
