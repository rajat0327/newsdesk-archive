Kotlin 2.4.20 Released

Kotlin 2.4.20 has been released, featuring coroutine stack trace recovery in the standard library and new collection utility functions. On the Kotlin/Native side, the update improves incremental compilation, enhances Swift export, and automates Package.swift generation for SwiftPM.

Why it matters — Coroutine stack trace recovery solves a major debugging pain point in asynchronous Kotlin code, while automatic Package.swift generation streamlines Kotlin Multiplatform integration into native iOS build pipelines.

iOS & Swift

Apple Opens Submissions for iOS 27, Deprecates Intel Macs

Apple has opened App Store submissions for apps built with Xcode 27 Release Candidate for iOS 27, iPadOS 27, macOS 27, tvOS 27, visionOS 27, and watchOS 27. macOS 27 will exclusively support Apple silicon, ending support for Intel Macs and Rosetta. Starting April 2027, new apps and updates must be built with the iOS 27 & iPadOS 27 SDK or later, and new age rating questions for social media capabilities are now mandatory for submissions.

Why it matters — Developers must update to Xcode 27, target arm64 only for macOS 27, and prepare for mandatory iOS 27 SDK adoption by April 2027; apps with social media features require new age rating answers this month.

ADB Wi-Fi 2.0 Boosts Wireless Debugging

Google has released ADB Wi-Fi 2.0, significantly improving wireless debugging for Android devices with up to 66% faster connection speeds and a 32% increase in automatic connection success rates. This update reworks the adb server, adbd daemon, and Android Studio, requiring Android 17 and Android SDK Platform-Tools 37.0.0.

Why it matters — Engineers must update devices to Android 17 and SDK Platform-Tools 37.0.0 to utilize the more reliable and faster wireless debugging capabilities.
