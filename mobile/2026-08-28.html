Update: New domain for Sign in with Apple

Apple is transitioning the domain used for Sign in with Apple private relay email addresses from privaterelay.appleid.com to private.icloud.com. While existing addresses will continue to work, new addresses will use the new domain, and iCloud+ Hide My Email addresses will remain on icloud.com.

Why it matters — Developers must update their email server configuration and allowlists to accept the new private.icloud.com domain to prevent communication failures with new users.
