How this works

Seven weekly engineering digests. Most of what you read here comes straight from an API, not from a language model.

Each channel gathers a week of stories, then publishes them here and to Discord as a slide deck. What differs between channels is how much a language model is trusted to do.

Release Radar uses no model at all: versions and end-of-life dates come from the GitHub Releases API and endoflife.date, so every number on the page is checkable. The security digest takes its exploited-vulnerability half straight from CISA's catalog. The reading and papers channels let a model choose and summarise, but never write a link or a title — those come from the feed. Only the news channels let a model find stories, and there every link is one it actually retrieved, filtered against a list of publications chosen in advance.

Where a story has nothing non-obvious to say, it says nothing rather than padding.
