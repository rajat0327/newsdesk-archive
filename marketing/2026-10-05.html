AI Visibility at Scale: Learning From 9M Prompts and 400+ Enterprise Brands

An analysis of 9 million prompts across 400 enterprise brands found that 82% of citations for commercial-intent queries go to third-party sites rather than owned brand pages. The research also discovered that sentiment and online reputation have a negligible or even negative correlation with earning AI citations.

Why it matters — Since 82% of commercial AI citations go to third parties, growth teams should prioritize external PR and mentions over owned content to win AI search visibility.

AI Overviews Currently Appear for 83% of Branded Searches

An investigation into AI Overviews (AIO) found that they now appear for 83% of branded search queries. This high frequency suggests that AI-generated summaries are increasingly intercepting traffic that previously went directly to a brand's own website.

Why it matters — With AIOs appearing on 83% of branded queries, brands must optimize for citations within the overview to avoid losing traffic to third-party sources like Wikipedia.

What’s Making AI Impact Difficult to Measure for Marketers?

Research indicates that brands recommended by ChatGPT are 2.5x more likely to receive a website visit, but over half of these visits are misattributed as organic search in analytics. This creates a 'dark AI' attribution gap where last-click models fail to identify the true source of the lead.

Why it matters — Because 50% of AI-influenced traffic appears as ordinary organic search, marketers must look for indirect signals rather than relying on last-click attribution to measure AI impact.

Google AI Pilot Pays Some Sites Under 0.1% Of Ad Revenue via @sejournal, @MattGSouthern

A report on Google's AI contribution pilot reveals that some participating publishers are receiving less than 0.1% of their typical ad revenue as compensation for AI-generated content usage. The study highlights significant discrepancies in payouts and a lack of transparency in calculation methods.

Why it matters — Early data shows AI publisher payouts are as low as 0.1% of ad revenue, suggesting AI-driven search may not be a viable revenue replacement for traditional ads.

Diagnosing AEO gaps: A content audit guide

This guide identifies 'AEO gaps'—reasons why AI engines fail to cite specific pages—such as information being buried too deep in text or the engine prioritizing third-party aggregators. It provides a framework for auditing content to ensure it is 'retrieval-ready' for semantic search.

Why it matters — AI engines often ignore primary sources if the answer isn't prominent; moving key information to the top of the page is now a technical requirement for AEO.
