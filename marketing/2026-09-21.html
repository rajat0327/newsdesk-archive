Google Auto-Migrates Search Campaigns to AI Max

Google began automatically migrating Search campaigns using Campaign-level Broad Match and Automatically Created Assets (ACA) to AI Max on September 1, 2026, continuing through the month. New Dynamic Search Ads (DSA) creation is blocked, with auto-migration planned for February 2027. Additionally, manual language targeting is being removed from Search and AI Max campaigns, with ad matching now based on ad and landing page language.

Why it matters — Advertisers must audit campaigns for AI Max migration, adapt to automatic language matching, and prepare for DSA deprecation by February 2027.
