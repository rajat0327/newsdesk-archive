Arista VeloCloud Orchestrator (VCO) on-prem contains an improper input validation vulnerability that may allow a remote attacker to access privileged internal functionality and impact the VCO host. Breaches

ShinyHunters Claims FBIJobs.gov Breach

The ShinyHunters cybercriminal group announced on September 22, 2026, that it compromised the FBI, defacing the FBIJobs.gov applicant portal and claiming to hold sensitive data on nearly every FBI agent and job applicant. The group alleged exploitation of a previously unknown vulnerability in Oracle PeopleSoft human resources software, while the FBI is investigating the claims.

Why it matters — Federal law enforcement personnel data, if genuinely exposed, carries risks beyond typical corporate leaks for agents working undercover or testifying.

The Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA) ordered U.S. government agencies to patch two critical Citrix NetScaler vulnerabilities (CVE-2026-88771 and CVE-2026-88772) by September 30, 2026, due to active exploitation. Malicious npm Package Evades Detection

A malicious npm package named 'indexed-btree' was found hiding its loader within application runtime code, rather than relying on traditional lifecycle scripts. Ransomware Attacks Hit Record High

Ransomware attacks reached a new high for 2026 in August, with NCC Group reporting 1,073 incidents globally, a 12% increase from July. The industrial sector was the most targeted, accounting for 31% of attacks, with Qilin and The Gentlemen being the most active threat groups.
