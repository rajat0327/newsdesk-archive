Supply Chain

Ethersphere Supply Chain Attack Steals Wallet Keys

Malicious versions of the `@ethersphere/bee-js` npm package (13.3.0) and Swarm Desktop app (0.56.2) were published on November 9, 2026, through the project's own release pipeline. The compromised code steals Swarm node wallet keys and installs backdoors on Windows, macOS, and Linux machines. Both malicious releases have been removed, and a clean Swarm Desktop update (0.56.3) was released.

Why it matters — Users who installed `bee-js` 13.3.0 or ran Swarm Desktop 0.56.2 must treat their machines as compromised, revoke affected credentials, and migrate to clean versions 13.2.0 and 0.56.3.

UK NCSC Warns AI Ransomware Threat to CNI

The UK National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC) published its Annual Review 2026 on November 3, 2026, warning that AI-powered malware is "highly likely" to be used in ransomware attacks against the UK's critical national infrastructure (CNI) by 2028. The report emphasizes AI's role in increasing the speed, scale, and efficiency of existing hostile operations.

Why it matters — UK critical infrastructure organizations must urgently invest in foundational cybersecurity and reduce internet exposure to counter AI-accelerated ransomware threats by 2028.
