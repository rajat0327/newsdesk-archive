Shifting HBM4 Controller Functions Closer To Memory (UC Riverside)

Research from UC Riverside explores controller partitioning strategies for HBM4 memory stacks. Because HBM4 transitions the stack's base die to an advanced logic process node, memory control functions can be split between the host SoC and the base die itself.

Why it matters — Using logic nodes for memory base dies allows migrating timing-critical memory control logic directly into the stack, freeing host SoC die area and simplifying routing.

DOJ, HHS Extend Web Accessibility Deadlines

The U.S. Department of Justice (DOJ) and the Department of Health and Human Services (HHS) have extended compliance deadlines for web and mobile accessibility requirements under ADA Title II and Section 504 of the Rehabilitation Act. Public entities with populations of 50,000 or more now have until April 2027 (DOJ) or May 11, 2027 (HHS for 15+ employees) to comply with WCAG 2.1 Level AA, with smaller entities and those with fewer employees receiving further extensions to April 2028 or May 10, 2028 respectively.

Why it matters — Organizations now have an additional year to achieve WCAG 2.1 Level AA compliance, reducing immediate litigation risk and allowing more thorough implementation of accessibility standards.

DPDP Act: Consent Manager Portal Opens

The Data Protection Board of India (DPBI) opened registration for Consent Managers under the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023, on November 13, 2026. The Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) launched an online portal for eligible Indian companies to apply. This marks the second phase of the DPDP Act's implementation, with remaining provisions effective May 13, 2027.

Why it matters — Companies processing personal data must now register as Consent Managers or integrate with registered ones, fundamentally changing how user consent is managed and audited.
