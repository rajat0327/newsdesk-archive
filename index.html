Eat Your Precision: Precision Objective Mitigates Reward Hacking in Rubric-based RL

Rubric-based rewards used during reinforcement learning on non-verifiable tasks primarily reward rubric coverage, turning RL into a recall-maximizing objective. Consequently, models suffer from reward hacking by producing overly verbose responses filled with unnecessary or incorrect facts. Incorporating an explicit precision objective alongside recall mitigates this failure mode.

Why it matters — Rubric-based RL rewards act as recall-only objectives that induce verbosity and hallucinations, which can be counteracted by adding a precision penalty.

The Ministry of Textiles reopened the application portal for Round 4 of the Production Linked Incentive (PLI) Scheme for Textiles from November 1 to December 31, 2026. Why it matters — Smaller manufacturing units (MSMEs) can now more easily access incentives to boost domestic production of Man-Made Fibre apparel, fabrics, and technical textiles.
