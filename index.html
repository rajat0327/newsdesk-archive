China crafts working 3nm gate-all-around transistors without EUV — stacked nanosheets target 3nm without EUV, but full node remains distant

Chinese researchers have successfully fabricated working 3nm gate-all-around (GAA) transistors using stacked nanosheets without relying on extreme ultraviolet (EUV) lithography. While this demonstrates a significant laboratory milestone, achieving a full, high-yield production node using alternative lithography remains a distant challenge.

Why it matters — Demonstrates that multi-patterning or alternative lithography techniques can physically define 3nm GAA structures, though scaling this to high-volume manufacturing without EUV remains economically and technically challenging.
