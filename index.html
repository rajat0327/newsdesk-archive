DPDP Act Clarifies Consent, Data Audit Terms

The Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) issued the Digital Personal Data Protection (Removal of Difficulties) Order, 2026, on October 5, 2026, effective upon gazette publication on October 7. The order clarifies Section 9(1) regarding verifiable consent for processing personal data of persons with disabilities who have lawful guardians, and amends Section 10(2)(c)(ii) by replacing 'audit' with 'data audit' for Significant Data Fiduciaries.

Why it matters — Businesses must ensure consent procedures and audit frameworks reflect the clarified language for persons with disabilities and explicitly use 'data audit' for SDF obligations.
