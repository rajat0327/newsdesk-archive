Time spent waiting to work is compensable if required, appeals court rules

A US appeals court ruled that employees must be paid for time spent waiting for a manager to unlock a store. The decision clarifies that such time is compensable under the FLSA if the employees are required to report at a specific time.

Why it matters — Time spent waiting under employer control is compensable, requiring time-tracking systems to account for 'pre-shift' waiting periods if employees are restricted from leaving.
