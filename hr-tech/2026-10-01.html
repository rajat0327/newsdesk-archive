Connecticut Enacts New HR Compliance Laws

Effective October 1, 2026, Connecticut's updated pay transparency requirements mandate employers include wage ranges and benefits descriptions in job advertisements and provide this information upon request. Employers with 100+ employees must also create and distribute a pay code guide. Additionally, expanded electronic monitoring notice requirements take effect, requiring disclosure of specific monitoring locations.

Why it matters — Connecticut employers must update job posting processes, create and distribute pay code guides, and revise electronic monitoring notices by October 1, 2026, to avoid non-compliance.
