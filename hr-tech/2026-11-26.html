Spain Enacts EU Pay Transparency Directive

Spain has implemented the EU Pay Transparency Directive through Royal Decree-Law 39/2026, effective November 19, 2026. This introduces significant changes to pay transparency, equal pay, and employee information rights, expanding remuneration register and pay audit obligations. Initial pay gap reporting deadlines are June 8, 2027, for employers with 150+ employees, and June 8, 2031, for those with 50-149 employees.

Why it matters — Employers in Spain must comply with new pay transparency rules from November 19, 2026, requiring updates to remuneration registers and pay audits with specific reporting deadlines.
