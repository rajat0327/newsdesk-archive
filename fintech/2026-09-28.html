FedNow Announces Support for Cross-Border Payments

The Federal Reserve is expanding FedNow to support cross-border payments, allowing banks to use the instant payments network for the US leg of international transactions. Initial use cases include international payroll, corporate payments, insurance claims, and treasury management, with correspondent banks handling the international portion.

Why it matters — Details the concrete expansion of a major real-time payment rail (FedNow) into cross-border capabilities, outlining specific use cases and the operational model involving correspondent banks for international transactions.

J.P. Morgan Payments Selects Thunes to Power Global Payout Solution

J.P. Morgan Payments partnered with Thunes to enhance its Xpedite Remit solution, integrating Thunes’ Direct Global Network for faster and more transparent cross-border payments. This collaboration provides J.P. Morgan access to 12 billion bank accounts and mobile wallets across over 100 corridors, reducing the need to establish local connectivity market by market.

Why it matters — Illustrates a strategic partnership model for scaling global payout solutions, highlighting the value of leveraging a network provider to gain access to a vast number of local payment endpoints (12 billion accounts/wallets) and reduce direct market-by-market integration efforts.
