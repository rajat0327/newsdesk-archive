RBI Allows UCBs to Fund IDPIC

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has amended investment guidelines, permitting Urban Co-operative Banks (UCBs) to invest in equity shares of the Indian Digital Payment Intelligence Corporation (IDPIC) for membership. IDPIC functions as a central digital payment fraud intelligence platform, with the amendment effective immediately.

Why it matters — UCBs must now allocate capital to IDPIC to access a centralized fraud intelligence platform, changing their fraud prevention strategy.

RBI Changes Calamity Relief Reporting

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has revised the reporting framework for Regulated Entities (REs) concerning relief measures in natural calamity-affected areas. Effective July 1, 2026, REs must submit details through the Centralised Information Management System (CIMS) portal on a half-yearly basis, by October 30 and April 30, discontinuing the previous monthly return.

Why it matters — REs must migrate from monthly to half-yearly CIMS reporting for calamity relief, adjusting internal processes for data collection and submission.
