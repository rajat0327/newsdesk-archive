Regulation

Hours After the GENIUS Act is Finalized, SoFi Launches Interest-Bearing Tokenized Deposit

SoFi launched SOFIE, an FDIC-eligible tokenized deposit offering 4.40% APY that converts 1:1 into SoFiUSD stablecoins for blockchain transfers. The roll-out directly follows final OCC regulations implementing the GENIUS Act, which imposes strict boundaries on non-bank stablecoin issuance.

Why it matters — Structuring tokenized deposits with 1:1 stablecoin conversions provides banks a compliant pathway to offer on-chain settlement while keeping assets within the FDIC-insured regulatory perimeter.

Payment Rails

US Faster Payments Council release guide on navigating non-value messaging for instant payments

The U.S. Faster Payments Council published an operational guide detailing non-value messaging standards across instant payment rails. The paper addresses technical flows, status query handling, and operational exceptions for non-financial signals.

Why it matters — Standardizing non-value messaging workflows like status inquiries and delivery confirmations prevents rail congestion and reduces exception handling overhead on real-time networks.

Fraud & Risk

Banks move to limit agents' access to customer accounts

Major Canadian banks including RBC and BMO are taking active measures to restrict autonomous AI agents from directly accessing customer bank accounts. Executives point to significant security and operational risks associated with third-party automated credential usage.

Why it matters — Bank-enforced blocks on autonomous agent access force fintech developers away from screen-scraping or credential sharing toward explicit, permissioned open banking API access.

Open Banking

U.S. Bank Taps Atomic to Simplify Recurring Payment Switching

U.S. Bank collaborated with Atomic to roll out Payment Switch, allowing account holders to programmatically transfer card-on-file billing across multiple merchants without manual credential updates. The system tracks recurring charges and automates subscription switching directly within the banking application.

Why it matters — Automated card-on-file switching via API integrations addresses primary-account acquisition friction by removing manual updates across merchant billing platforms.

Trends from Money20/20 2026: The rise of “hidden” financial services

Money20/20 2026 identified three infrastructure‑driven trends: stablecoins becoming a de‑facto settlement layer, the expansion of agent‑based distribution models, and the emergence of "always‑on" money where financial services are embedded and invisible to end users.

Why it matters — Embedding services through agents and stablecoins cuts friction and expands reach, a reusable pattern for building scalable payment experiences.
