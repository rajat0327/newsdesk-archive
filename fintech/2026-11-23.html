Payment Rails

Give your agents controlled access to analyze your data and move funds

Stripe Treasury introduced controls enabling autonomous AI agents to initiate fund transfers, convert currencies, and pay vendors. The platform allows businesses to set granular operational limits and mandate human approvals before payments execute.

Why it matters — Enabling programmatic money movement for AI agents requires policy-enforced execution caps and human-in-the-loop authorization controls at the API layer.

Fraud & Risk

Feedzai’s New Tool Detects AI Agents Accessing Customer Accounts

Feedzai updated its Digital Trust solution to help banks detect and manage third-party AI agents accessing customer accounts. The tool uses cryptographic device binding, device fingerprinting, network intelligence, and behavioral analytics to separate authorized automated AI traffic from potential account takeover attacks.

Why it matters — Securing customer accounts against unauthorized AI agent access requires cryptographic device binding combined with behavioral telemetry rather than relying solely on traditional user credentials.

Regulation

Mexico Relaxes Card Fee Rules to Promote Digital Payments

Mexico is introducing revised caps on card processing fees to encourage digital payment adoption across the country. The government proposal phases in yearly average caps reaching 1% on credit card transactions and 0.3% on debit card transactions by 2029.

Why it matters — Phased interchange fee caps reaching 1% for credit and 0.3% for debit by 2029 establish the baseline revenue model for payment processing in Mexico.

Payment Rails

Starling Bank Brings Conversational AI to Everyday Payments

Starling Bank is introducing a voice-driven payment capability powered by Starling Intelligence for retail account holders by the end of 2026. Customers can initiate transfer prompts using natural language while retaining explicit final manual authorization step prior to settlement.

Why it matters — Decoupling intent initiation via natural language from mandatory manual step-up approval reduces transaction friction without compromising user authorization security.

GTCR Weighs Another Bid for Global Payments

Private equity firm GTCR submitted an SEC filing confirming it is assessing a acquisition bid for payment processing provider Global Payments. The potential transaction indicates ongoing private equity appetite for taking large-scale legacy payment acquirers private.
