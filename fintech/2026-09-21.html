Regulation

OCC Opens Three Bank Doors to Stablecoins and AI Agents

The Office of the Comptroller of the Currency (OCC) has issued conditional approvals for three new national trust banks: Agora National Trust Bank, Catena Trust Bank, and Bastion Platforms Trust Company. These approvals allow the institutions to operate with stablecoins and AI agents under direct federal oversight.

Why it matters — Establishes a concrete regulatory precedent for national trust banks to natively integrate stablecoins and agentic AI, signaling a shift from fintech-reliant models to direct regulatory oversight.

Baselayer Partners with Nevermined to Bring Identity Verification to Agentic Payments

Business identity and risk intelligence firm Baselayer has partnered with agentic payments infrastructure company Nevermined. The partnership integrates identity verification directly into agentic payments, allowing AI agents to obtain the necessary authority to execute transactions on behalf of users.

Why it matters — Solves a key architectural bottleneck in agentic commerce by linking business identity verification directly to autonomous payment authorization.

Open Banking

Plaid Taps ID.me to Help Government Agencies Verify Financial Beneficiaries and their Accounts

Plaid and ID.me have partnered to help US government agencies verify both benefit recipients' identities and ownership of the financial accounts receiving payments. The partnership combines identity and account verification into a single flow to reduce manual processes, duplicate payments, and fraud.

Why it matters — Demonstrates the efficiency gains of combining KYC/identity verification with open-banking account ownership verification in a single API flow to prevent disbursement fraud.
