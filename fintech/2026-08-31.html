Swift accepts request to extend structured address migration for ISO 20022 messages

Swift has extended the deadline for migrating from unstructured to structured postal addresses in ISO 20022 payment messages. This extension was granted after numerous banks requested additional time to prepare their systems for compliance.

Core Banking

Deutsche Bank's Private Bank selects Thought Machine for radical core banking shakeup

Deutsche Bank's Private Bank has selected Thought Machine's Vault Core to consolidate its core banking infrastructure. The migration aims to reduce the bank's total number of core systems from 15 down to just two modern, cloud-based platforms.

Why it matters — It demonstrates a major tier-1 bank's willingness to accept the immense migration risk of replacing 15 legacy cores in exchange for the operational simplicity of a unified, cloud-native ledger.

Cross-Border

Bank of America Says APAC Redefines Cross-Border Money Movement

Bank of America reports that the rapid adoption of real-time domestic payment rails in the Asia-Pacific region is forcing cross-border transactions to match their 24/7 speed. This acceleration creates operational challenges for US corporations operating on traditional, non-continuous treasury schedules.

Why it matters — The mismatch between 24/7 domestic real-time rails and traditional, batch-based cross-border settlement windows forces corporate treasurers to redesign their liquidity and FX management workflows.

Cross-Border

Nium Debuts USDC Funding for Client Payouts

Cross-border payments platform Nium has launched a stablecoin-based funding option, allowing businesses to fund their accounts with USDC and execute payouts in local fiat currencies. This setup aims to eliminate the need for businesses to prefund local currency accounts across Nium's payout network.

Why it matters — Utilizing stablecoins as a settlement layer solves the capital-inefficiency problem of prefunding multiple local fiat accounts, offering a practical hybrid model for global payouts.

Payment Rails

Bluefin Teams with Visa for In-Person Payment Acceptance

Bluefin has partnered with Visa to integrate its PCI-validated point-to-point encryption (P2PE) with Visa Acceptance Solutions. This collaboration aims to secure card-present, in-person payment acceptance for merchants, software providers, and enterprises.

Why it matters — Combining point-to-point encryption (P2PE) directly with card-brand acceptance networks simplifies the deployment of secure, PCI-compliant physical payment terminals for software platforms.

Stripe Acquires Clerky to Offer Legal Infrastructure for Startups

Stripe has acquired Clerky, a legal infrastructure provider for startups, to expand its services into company formation, fundraising, and hiring workflows. This acquisition allows Stripe to integrate deeper legal workflows alongside its existing Stripe Atlas tool.

Why it matters — Acquiring legal and incorporation infrastructure allows payment processors to capture customer relationships at the point of company formation, long before they process their first transaction.

Open Banking

CFPB Open Banking Rule Stalled; States Consider Own Mandates

As of August 2026, the CFPB's Section 1033 Personal Financial Data Rights rule, finalized in October 2024, remains blocked by a federal court injunction issued on October 29, 2025. The CFPB is currently rewriting the rule, revisiting key issues such as consumer representation, data access fees, security, and privacy. The initial compliance date of April 1, 2026, passed without implementation. In response to the federal delay, New York state lawmakers introduced bills in March 2026 that would ban data-access fees at the state level.

Why it matters — Banks and fintechs must monitor state-level open banking legislation, like New York's proposed data-access fee ban, due to the federal Section 1033 rule's ongoing injunction.
