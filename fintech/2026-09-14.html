Singapore banks complete blockchain-enabled SGD transactions on Swift ledger

DBS, OCBC, and UOB have successfully executed live domestic Singapore dollar transactions using tokenized deposits on Swift's blockchain-based ledger.

Why it matters — Demonstrates that existing global messaging networks like Swift can be leveraged for multi-bank tokenized deposit settlement, avoiding the need to build entirely new proprietary networks.

Blik records first agentic payment transaction

Polish mobile payment network Blik has successfully completed its first live agentic payment transaction, where a user delegated purchasing authority to an AI agent to find and pay for a product.

Why it matters — Demonstrates a shift from synchronous user-initiated checkouts to asynchronous, delegated agentic payments, which requires new risk, consent, and pre-authorization frameworks.

Late B2B Payments Tax 4.1% of Corporate Revenue

Research from PYMNTS Intelligence reveals that late B2B payments cost corporate revenue an average of 4.1%, prompting leading finance departments to treat liquidity as a proactive asset.

Why it matters — Quantifies the financial penalty of inefficient B2B payment cycles at 4.1% of revenue, justifying the business case for automated working capital and real-time treasury tools.

Mastercard’s Sherri Haymond on Letting AI Shop While Consumers Stay in Charge

Mastercard is exploring the integration of agentic AI in commerce, where AI agents can navigate merchant sites and use payment credentials to complete purchases. The focus is on establishing secure delegation of authority so AI can execute transactions while keeping consumers in control.

Why it matters — Delegating payment credentials to autonomous AI agents requires new security frameworks to manage cross-platform authorization without compromising consumer control.

India & UPI

RBI Launches UPI Tap & Pay, AI Support

RBI Governor Sanjay Malhotra unveiled UPI 'Tap & Pay' for NFC-based POS payments and MyUPI, an AI-powered customer support platform built on NPCI's FiMI, at Global Fintech Fest 2026. Tap & Pay allows PIN-less transactions up to Rs 5,000, while MyUPI offers consolidated transaction views and safety features like a Safety Switch.

Why it matters — Users gain faster, PIN-less NFC payments up to ₹5,000 and AI-powered tools for transaction oversight, fraud reporting, and mobile number delinking.

Nu Launches US Banking, Stablecoin Payments

Latin American digital bank Nu launched its product suite in the US, including a multi-currency digital account, Nu Global. It offers fee-free local and international payments, converting deposits into USDC and EURC stablecoins for transfers across over 35 countries.

Why it matters — US customers can now access fee-free international payments to 35+ countries, leveraging stablecoins for faster, cheaper cross-border settlement.
