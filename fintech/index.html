OCC Opens Three Bank Doors to Stablecoins and AI Agents

The Office of the Comptroller of the Currency (OCC) has issued conditional approvals for three new national trust banks: Agora National Trust Bank, Catena Trust Bank, and Bastion Platforms Trust Company. These approvals allow the institutions to operate with stablecoins and AI agents under direct federal oversight.

Why it matters — Establishes a concrete regulatory precedent for national trust banks to natively integrate stablecoins and agentic AI, signaling a shift from fintech-reliant models to direct regulatory oversight.
