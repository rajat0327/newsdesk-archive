India & UPI

Indian Consumers Embrace Cash Ahead of New Instant Payment Fees

India's UPI system is introducing a fee for transactions exceeding 2,000 rupees (~$21) starting next month. This regulatory shift is already driving a measurable increase in cash usage among consumers as they look to avoid the new digital transaction costs.

Why it matters — The 2,000-rupee threshold serves as a psychological and economic pivot point where transaction fees begin to outweigh the convenience of real-time digital payments.

Bank of America deployed 1,000 contractors alongside 3,000 internal staff to remediate software vulnerabilities identified by Anthropic’s Mythos AI model. This massive manual effort was required to patch flaws at a scale that exceeded the bank's standard internal capacity.

HKEX sets T+1 transition roadmap

Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing (HKEX) has confirmed a transition roadmap to a T+1 settlement cycle for the cash market. The move follows a consultation period showing broad market support for accelerated settlement to reduce counterparty risk.

Why it matters — Moving to T+1 requires significant changes to post-trade automation and liquidity management to handle the compressed settlement window without increasing fail rates.
