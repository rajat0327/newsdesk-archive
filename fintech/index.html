Why global workers are driving demand for stablecoin payouts

Stripe surveyed 2,300 workers across 20 countries to analyze the growing demand for stablecoin payouts. The study highlights how platforms like DoorDash, Meta, and Deel are implementing stablecoin infrastructure to facilitate faster, cheaper cross-border payments for global workforces.

Why it matters — Highlights stablecoins transitioning from speculative assets to practical B2C settlement infrastructure for global gig-economy platforms.

Crypto Card Spending Jumps Threefold in a Year, Paymentscan Data Shows

Cryptocurrency card spending reached $1.04 billion in July, representing a threefold year-over-year increase. Data indicates that dollar-backed stablecoins drove 70% of the more than 10 million tracked retail transactions, signaling a shift toward stablecoins for everyday purchases.

Why it matters — Demonstrates that stablecoins, rather than volatile cryptocurrencies, are driving the actual retail adoption of crypto-linked payment cards.

Standard Chartered issues digitally native notes on Euroclear D-FMI

Standard Chartered has issued $200 million in three-year floating-rate digitally native notes (DNNs) on Euroclear's Digital Financial Market Infrastructure (D-FMI). This transaction marks the first time a Global Systemically Important Bank (G-SIB) and a UK issuer have utilized Euroclear's digital debt issuance platform.

Why it matters — Marks a concrete milestone in institutional debt tokenization, moving digital financial market infrastructure (D-FMI) from pilot to a $200 million live issuance.

Wall Street’s New Cybersecurity Threat Starts With a Phone Call

Google's Threat Intelligence Group reports that threat actor UNC6671 is targeting financial firms by exploiting cloud-based identity platforms and SaaS ecosystems. Instead of traditional network breaches, attackers are using social engineering to log in directly to collaboration suites.

Why it matters — Attackers are shifting from 'breaking in' to 'logging in' via identity providers, necessitating a move toward identity-centric security over traditional network perimeters.
